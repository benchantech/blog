# Ben Chan Tech — AI-Native Company Constitution

## Mission

Ben Chan Tech finds the practical boundary between human judgment and AI execution by running real systems, preserving the evidence, and publishing what survives.

## The $20 rule

The core public company must remain buildable, publishable, and operable with one ChatGPT Plus subscription as its only required AI operating expense.

Additional AI subscriptions, paid inference APIs, agent platforms, vector databases, AI CMS products, and other paid AI services may be used only for optional bounded experiments. They may not become required infrastructure for the company’s core public knowledge surface.

## Authority

Ben sets direction, remains accountable, and owns consequential judgment.

AI may execute autonomously inside delegated authority. AI may not expand its own authority.

Escalate only when work crosses an authority boundary: consequential judgment, genuine ambiguity with material downstream impact, irreversible external action, money or contract commitment, unsupported public claim, or conflict with current governing evidence.

Do not escalate ordinary implementation choices merely because they are choices.

## Operating preference

Use ChatGPT first for research, synthesis, simulation, planning, writing, content architecture, evidence mapping, review, and implementation work that can be expressed as ordinary code or content.

Use GitHub as durable company state, evidence registry, work history, and publication substrate.

Use specialist coding tools only when runtime engineering, broad refactoring, local build execution, browser debugging, or environment-specific verification materially requires them.

If ChatGPT can produce the final artifact directly, do not add another AI tool.

## Public evidence

AI may search, organize, pressure-test, simulate, and synthesize Ben’s record. It may not manufacture Ben’s experience, authority, historical facts, present judgment, or source provenance.

Synthetic simulations must be labeled as simulations and cannot substitute for real cases.

Public claims should prefer evidence from real work, observable outcomes, dated operating records, and inspectable artifacts.

## Publishing

Every indexed answer surface should stand alone as useful to a human reader and also strengthen the surrounding evidence graph.

No indexed page should exist merely because AI can generate a variation. A page earns publication by answering a materially distinct question with identifiable evidence.

History is append-only. Current interpretations may change; the record of what happened may not be silently rewritten.
